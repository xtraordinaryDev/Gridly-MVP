High level
GridLink is the fuel management software connecting organizations that purchase fuel with a verified network of qualified fuel suppliers across the $6.4 trillion global oil industry. Our software enables businesses, farmers, hospitals, transportation providers, government agencies, airports, construction companies, and other fuel buyers to discover qualified fuel suppliers, compare bids, manage contracts, and streamline the procurement process. Since 2025, GridLink has secured $2.3 million in signed fuel contracts and processed more than 1 million gallons of fuel.

Product
GridLink centralizes fuel procurement in one platform. Buyers access a verified supplier network, launch sourcing events or source fuel directly, use AI to compare price, qualifications, availability, and value, award business, and monitor contracts and fuel activity in real time. A live MVP is available for demonstration and product testing.
