High level
GridLink is the AI procurement platform for bulk fuel. A buyer describes what they need in one sentence, GridLink AI drafts the RFP, ranks verified suppliers and briefs the buyer on every bid, and the buyer makes the award. This month we signed our first supplier, moved to gridlink.global, and shipped the three AI features that make the product different. The product is ready. The job now is getting fuel buyers and distributors onto it.
