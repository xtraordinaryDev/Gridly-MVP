Starting in November
We are preparing for three enterprise fuel contracts that begin in November. The largest is at an airport: during snowstorms it needs 15,000 gallons of fuel every four hours to keep its de-icing trucks running. A storm does not wait for a purchase order, so the work right now is lining up supplier capacity and dispatch coverage ahead of the first snow, and making sure every delivery is scheduled, ticketed and invoiced through GridLink from day one.

Growing the network
Beyond November, we are actively onboarding more enterprise buyers and suppliers every week. Each new supplier goes through the same verification: company profile, W-9, certificate of insurance and licenses, reviewed by our team before they can bid. Each new buyer brings their sites and their fuel needs, and the AI starts matching from the first RFP.
